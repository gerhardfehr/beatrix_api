## Summary Statistics

### Confirmed (N=6)
- **Mean B**: 0.86 (strong claims)
- **Mean E**: 0.75 (good evidence)
- **Mean K**: 0.90 (well-calibrated)
- **Domain**: Mostly cognitive psychology
- **Original N**: Well-powered studies

### Failed (N=8)
- **Mean B**: 0.87 (strong claims)
- **Mean E**: 0.53 (weak evidence)
- **Mean K**: 0.65 (poorly calibrated)
- **Domain**: Mostly social priming
- **Original N**: Often N<100

## Key Patterns

### 🎯 What Replicates
- **Cognitive > Social**: Memory, perception, attention effects replicate
- **Larger N**: Well-powered original studies
- **Controlled**: Within-subjects, lab paradigms
- **Decades**: Long history of replication

### ❌ What Fails
- **Social priming**: 6 of 8 failures are priming effects
- **Small N**: Original N < 50 → 0% replication rate
- **Single lab**: No independent replications pre-publication
- **Publication bias**: P-values clustered at .05

### 📊 ESL Insights
- **Overclaiming**: Failed studies used definitive language despite weak evidence
- **K < 0.75**: All failed replications had poor calibration
- **Appropriate hedging**: Only facial feedback (broad) used "can" → survived despite specific failure

## ESL Interpretation Guide

### B-value (Claim Strength)
- **0.90+**: "causes," "proves," "demonstrates" → Very strong
- **0.80-0.89**: "shows," "indicates" → Strong
- **0.60-0.79**: "suggests," "supports" → Moderate
- **0.40-0.59**: "may," "could" → Tentative

### E-value (Evidence Quality)
- **0.85+**: Meta-analysis, triangulated
- **0.70-0.84**: Well-powered RCTs, preregistered
- **0.55-0.69**: Lab experiments, adequate N
- **0.40-0.54**: Small N (<100), single lab
- **<0.40**: Very small N (<50), underpowered

### K-value (Calibration)
- **K ≥ 0.80**: Stable, well-calibrated
- **K 0.60-0.79**: Partial, needs revision
- **K < 0.60**: Poor, major miscalibration

---

## Recommendations

### ✅ For Well-Calibrated Claims (K ≥ 0.75)
1. Continue using evidence-matched language
2. Document sample characteristics
3. Encourage independent replication

### ⚠️ For Poorly Calibrated Claims (K < 0.75)
1. **Strengthen evidence**: Preregister, increase N, replicate internally
2. **Weaken claims**: Add hedging ("may," "suggests," "preliminary")
3. **Acknowledge limits**: WEIRD samples, single paradigm, exploratory

### 🔍 Red Flags for Reviewers
- Strong claims (B > 0.85) + weak evidence (E < 0.60) = K < 0.75
- Original N < 50 with definitive language
- Social priming without preregistration
- No internal replication before publication
